B1 speaking game

WHO AM I?

Spin the celebrity wheel, keep your identity secret and let your partner guess!

YOUR CELEBRITY IS...Ready to play?Your celebrity will appear here.

How to play

  1. One student spins the wheel and secretly looks at the celebrity.
  2. The other student asks YES/NO questions to guess the identity.
  3. You have a maximum of 10 questions.

Challenge: Use at least five different tenses while asking questions.
